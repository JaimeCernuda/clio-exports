## Aim

Make SPOTTER's investigation understandable through changing evidence, a persistent
dependency graph, recorded review activity, and word-level attention. Keep the
approved transparent-logo introduction and the source-ordered main conversation.
This is a technical demonstration, not a capability advertisement.

## Source contract

Chain B 7795459 supplies six prompts containing eighteen precomputed lookup-result
packets. The primary agent makes **no actual tool calls**. The graph reconstructs
that scenario from exact prompt spans and actual answers; it is not Flowcept
telemetry. SPOTTER's seven recorded tools retain their original call IDs.
Source SHA and identities remain in the paired replay. Attention is measured data,
not a verdict or a demonstration of causality.
