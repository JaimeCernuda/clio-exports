## User feedback addressed

The early wheel became too blurred; actual prompt edits were hard to distinguish;
skills/hooks/MCP changes were hidden inside short-lived disclosures; dotted count
summaries added noise; and every plugin caused the prompt to shrink, grow and
recenter again.

The wheel now uses vertical-only blur with a short exposure driven by velocity,
a motion threshold and a 4 px cap. At the fourth field its blur is under 1 px.
Holds have zero blur, and it clears as the wheel slows. The opening still uses
one vertical wheel, not a second typing device.

The prompt stays fixed throughout composition: x 140, y 245, unscaled size
1240 × 620 at scale 0.82. Its visible rectangle is 1016.8 × 508.4. Actual inserted
and removed fragments receive their owner's pastel color; stable instructions
remain unmarked. The tint stays through the reading settle and then fades.

A persistent tree connects the prompt to plugin roots and their owned Skills,
MCP servers, Tools and Hooks leaves. Additions reveal branches in sequence.
Domain replacements withdraw the outgoing capabilities and reveal different
names beneath the incoming plugin. Reports and its leaves remain connected and
move as a complete subtree. All dotted capability-count summaries are removed.
Simulation and Reports still get visibly clicked before their effects appear.

The old inspection/collapse/dock/refocus loop is removed. The full cut is shorter
because those repeated movements are gone. The final QR remains settled for five
seconds. No changes to the illustrative-versus-recorded evidence boundary were made.
