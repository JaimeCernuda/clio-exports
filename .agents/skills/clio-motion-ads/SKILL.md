# Product motion advertisements

Make one product capability understandable through a visible transformation.
The subject and viewer takeaway come from the brief. A scientific case can support
that claim when useful; it does not automatically become the subject.

Read [the lessons from the composability ad](references/lessons.md) before choosing
the treatment. For revisions to that specific film, use its
[focused skill](../../../composability/ad/skills/clio-composable-ad/SKILL.md),
[handoff](../../../composability/ad/HANDOFF.md) and
[runnable project](../../../composability/ad/README.md). Its wheel, fixed prompt
and plugin tree are one treatment, not the required layout for future ads.

## Establish a visual argument

For the eight-film capability campaign, use its [entrypoint](../../../ads/capabilities/README.md)
and [source/review contract](../../../ads/capabilities/REPRODUCE.md). Read the relevant
film's own storyboard before changing its timing. Its editable pieces are deliberately
not constrained to CLIO's app window. See [capability campaign lessons](references/capability-lessons.md)
when adapting those compositions or addressing idle controls and map distortion.

State the audience, claim, visible proof and desired next action before arranging
screens. Turn the user's rough examples into a coherent concept; they are useful
direction, not a requirement to animate every suggested gesture. Choose the
strongest actions and explain what each changes for the viewer.

For this user's short social ads, aim to land the hook within roughly four to five
seconds. Avoid spending ten seconds listing possibilities before showing the idea.
Choose one opening device and give it a readable beginning, a deliberate pace
change and a clear resolution. Do not combine a typing/deleting effect and a wheel
for the same words. A different subject can use a different device.

When using a craft reference, inspect the actual work and identify the principles
being adapted: hierarchy, object continuity, pacing or product-driven transitions.
Record those sources and your interpretation in the campaign's art-direction note.
Competitor-like polish is not a substitute for a concept specific to this product.

## Compose recognizable product pieces

Recreate the relevant product elements as editable components. Reflow, reshape,
scale or isolate them for the film. Remove app navigation, metadata and technical
scaffolding that do not explain the claim. A full app shell or a static two-column
documentation layout usually makes this user's ad read as a presentation demo.

Give the viewer one visual priority at a time. Introduce the cause before showing
its consequence. If a gesture is shown, the control and resulting state must agree.
Opening a chooser, selecting an item and applying its effect are separate events.
Do not show an unselected plugin in the active tree before its selection.
Interaction is useful when it reveals an effect; arbitrary text selections,
decorative clicks and repeated zooms add little.

Preserve product semantics while simplifying presentation. For composition, plugins
own prompt fragments, skills, tools, hooks and MCP declarations. A domain change
updates identity; an additive capability need not replace it. Show contributions
entering or leaving with their owner. Color actual changed content, rather than
merely recoloring its section. A declaration is not proof of a live connection.
Keep illustrative copy and source provenance documented outside the film.

## Choreograph through occupied space

Drive visuals, hover feedback, cursor presses and sound from the same frame clock.
Measure real fonts and wrapping before rendering. Scope word matches to their
source fragment; preserve stable text, make room for insertions and clear removals
before retained text moves through them. Avoid overlapping readable paragraphs.

Keep a selected object continuous through a handoff: its first detached frame
should retain its source rectangle and typography. Retire neighboring contents
before expanding it. Check whole-object travel paths as well as individual words.
Clip or unmount hidden content; transformed descendants can paint beyond their
intended bounds in an encoded render.

If using an accelerated wheel, shorten the change intervals along a smooth curve.
Derive directional blur from cadence and movement; early words remain readable
and the resolved word is sharp. Do not fade straight into full blur. Budget holds
after the last useful row or text morph has finished, not from the start of its
reveal. Avoid repeating an elaborate inspection for every item.

When a cursor is part of the story, keep its route continuous and above the content.
Move it out through the frame edge between gestures. Measure its hotspot against
the actual rendered control at each press, including entry and exit speed. Animate
hover explicitly: an SVG cursor does not cause browser CSS hover. Keep add,
confirmation and remove states distinct under the cursor.

## Review and hand off

Review the moving encode around entrances, selections, edits, transfers and the
close. Check consecutive frames for flicker, leaking text, collisions and abrupt
state changes. Review normal-speed pacing and sound when playback is available;
state exactly what was inspected if only sampled frames or technical checks were
possible. Passing tests and clean stills do not establish creative approval.

Use the actual supplied logos with their transparency. Keep the closing action
clear, make URL/QR legible when requested, and allow a stationary scanning hold.
Credits belong below the main message. Current composability branding and GRC
artwork are recorded in its handoff; they are not mandatory bookends for every ad.

Group each campaign's source, copy/timing, assets, render commands, sound, provenance
and review notes together. Give another agent a clear entrypoint and document any
shared source dependencies. Reuse the existing rendering and text-morph helpers
where useful; do not overwrite a previous campaign to start a new subject.
Website loops should render their editable scenes directly and use the branding
appropriate to the host page, with a clean repeat and reduced-motion poster.

On this machine, keep dependencies, caches, temporary files and outputs on D:.
Retain the latest delivered final, committed review history, original sessions and
current website outputs. Prune superseded ad cuts and redundant intermediates
under the user's cleanup authorization after checking the latest final. Report a
blocked cleanup action honestly and leave that target alone. Follow the repository's
current publishing instructions; this skill itself does not authorize a push.
