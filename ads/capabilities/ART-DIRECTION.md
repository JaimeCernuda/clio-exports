# The campaign's visual argument

The audience should think: **I could use this in my work, and build on it.**
CLIO is the subject. Scientific results make its capabilities concrete without
turning a short ad into a science lecture. Each film is understandable muted.
The brief asks for landscape 1080p60, a hook within four to five seconds, original
sound, concise copy and a five-second stationary invitation to collaborate.

The user's repeated distinction matters: a marketing ad may isolate, reshape,
reflow and choreograph recognizable product elements. It does not need a window,
tabs, navigation or a generic two-column technical-demo arrangement. No toolbars
are inherited merely because a component can draw them.

## Eight motion arguments

1. A written procedure spreads into connected work. A result travels through it;
   an unsuccessful review sends it back through the relevant calculation.
2. Two curves agree, then separate. The discrepancy creates linked scientific
   evidence and changes the claim in the graph.
3. A connected investigation grows through preparation, concurrent specialists,
   a join and a conditional follow-up. One nested flow opens while its peers
   continue. The same graph persists through the finding, without a layout swap.
4. An answer becomes an instrument. Baseline and month choices visibly update
   a chart and its linked numeric comparison before export.
5. A result can be followed backward, through a calculation and geometry to its
   source, then forward again without losing its identity.
6. Sources and tools assemble around a program. Neighbors clear before its useful
   result expands. Source references remain with the result.
7. Geographic observations remain the same selected records across a map, table,
   reference card, actual record read, computation and answer. The map never
   squashes to accommodate another view; place names remain stable DOM text.
8. The signal keeps arriving. A task supplies burn observations to both an agent
   query and a dashboard. Those same wind readings drive an illustrative parcel
   transport model; its output joins its inputs in context. The rejected unrelated
   pendulum switch is removed. Multiple data sources must belong to one visible story.

The neutral background, Inter typography, navy brand, controlled accent palette,
light borders and close connect the campaign. Subject-specific motion supplies
the distinction. Neither the composability wheel nor its plugin tree is reused.

## Craft references and interpretation

- [BUCK / Google Vids](https://buck.co/work/google-vids): product behavior can be
  the source of a motion language. Here, connected execution, graph growth and
  reference continuity are the arguments, rather than decorative transitions.
- [BUCK / Notion](https://buck.co/work/notion-think-it-make-it): familiar interface
  fragments can become graphic material while retaining recognizable meaning.
- [Oddfellows / Figma](https://oddfellows.tv/work/figma): change hierarchy through
  choreography and layout; retain enough stable space to understand the change.

These references inform hierarchy and continuity, not copied assets or scenes.
Scientific curves, source records, computations and task completion require their
own evidence. Original audio is synthesized locally; no stock track is copied.

## Changes from the first assembly

The user rejected the universal CLIO window treatment, the compressed track with
no geographic background, and a toolbar on the gene-count result that did nothing.
The current source removes the default window chrome and idle actions, uses a
fixed-scale regional Mercator map with real Natural Earth geography, and lets the
gene-count result stand on its content. These are systemic composition changes,
not a justification to add more cursor gestures.
