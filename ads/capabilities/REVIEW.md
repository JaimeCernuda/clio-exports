## Encode and visual review

Current per-encode technical results live in local `review/*-technical.json`.
Time-ordered whole-film sheets and dense cue-transition sheets are extracted from
the actual encoded files, not source-only screenshots. Individual source stills
and DOM bounds are additional checks. Exact visual findings and inspected versions
are recorded in `review-history.json` as rendering proceeds.

This document is not creative approval. Inspection coverage must distinguish
sampled consecutive frames, normal-speed browser playback, and sound review.
Uninterrupted audiovisual human review cannot be inferred from image sheets.
The technical checks also reject silent/clipping audio. This is signal-level
verification, not a subjective sound-mix review. Per-version presentation limits
are shown in the gallery and retained in the local delivery manifest.
