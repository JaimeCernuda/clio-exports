# The organizations behind these NDP records

Checked against the captured catalog responses and primary publisher records on 2026-10-28.

| Case  | Who produced or published it                                                                                                                             | What the film can accurately say                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| ----- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Tahoe | Tahoe Regional Planning Agency (NDP organization `trpa`)                                                                                                 | TRPA publishes a lake boundary delineation. CLIO finds it through NDP, measures the polygon and explains its limits. The polygon is not a current water-level survey.                                                                      |
| Alta  | Michael L. Wasserstein and Jim Steenburgh; published by The Hive, University of Utah Research Data Repository (NDP organization `marriott_library_hive`) | The researchers share 12-hour manual snow and liquid-precipitation observations from Alta-Collins. CLIO combines the 2023–24 record with the 2000–23 record and checks coverage before comparing winters.                                  |
| Konza | NEON Mobile Deployment Platform and Sage, working with Konza Prairie Biological Station; dataset published in WIFIRE Commons under `sage-neon`           | The April 15, 2022 prescribed-burn experiment produced multiple sensor streams. CLIO compares particles, wind and CO₂, retaining sampling gaps and sensor limitations. The selected session begins with the data already in its workspace. |

NDP makes these published collections discoverable. It is not the original producer of every observation. Organization names in the film identify sources; they are not endorsements.

Primary references:

- [Alta 2023–24 DOI](https://doi.org/10.7278/S5d-dx7x-d8ay) and [Alta 2000–23 DOI](https://doi.org/10.7278/S50d-nsy5-8bje). DataCite metadata confirms both named creators, publisher and observation method. NDP records `16bee949-c829-4038-a316-204a5322b7d8` and `eb9652c3-6825-4cfa-93ad-31a4cff0607c`.
- [About The Hive](https://hive.utah.edu/about?locale=en): provided by the Marriott and Eccles libraries to preserve and share University of Utah research data and code.
- [NEON MDP / Sage / WIFIRE BP3D Konza dataset](https://wifire-data.sdsc.edu/dataset/neon-mdp-sage-wifire-bp3d-konza-prairie-burn-experiment): identifies the collaboration, collection date, sensor resources, author Ismael Perez and DOI `10.57719/S54W2D`. NDP record `335fa1cb-d6d2-4e96-bc8e-c309d957b24d`.

Do not substitute Alta resort marketing totals or the separate Alta Guard station record for Alta-Collins. Do not label the Konza optical particle estimate as verified PM2.5 or use the observed correlations as causal attribution.
