How Granite splits the demo text

Each coloured block is one token from the Granite 4.2 8B tokenizer. Words underlined in amber were split into two or more tokens. Spaces are shown as · and line breaks as ↵. Hover a token to see its ID.

Source text is from Polaris Chain B (run 7795459), round 5, as recorded in the replay export. The planted instruction line in the tool output is left out of this page.

Each sample is tokenized on its own. Inside the full 46,463-token prompt, the same text gets the same tokens except possibly at the first and last token of a sample.

"Grouped by word" joins neighbouring pieces made only of letters, digits and underscores, with no space between them. It is a display preview and does not change the tokens.
